Try Saying

"Hey Jarvis, Priya ko call karo" — calls a contact, Hindi word order

"Jarvis, what's the weather?" — live weather for your location

"Jarvis, meri sister ko whatsapp pe sms karo ki khana kya banaya hai" — opens WhatsApp with the message ready to send

Features

🎙️

Offline voice commands

Calls, SMS, apps, flashlight, volume, alarms — all work in airplane mode.

🧠

Auto Learn Mode

Learns your habits over time and proactively suggests helpful actions.

👁️

On-device vision

Reads text (OCR), recognizes objects and faces — no photo ever leaves your phone.

🔒

Voice authentication

Optional voice-lock so only your voice can run commands.

📱

Home screen widget

One tap to start listening — no need to open the app.

💬

WhatsApp integration

Compose WhatsApp messages by voice, in Hindi or English.

Why Offline-First?

Most voice assistants send everything to the cloud. Jarvis handles the vast majority of commands entirely on-device — your calls, contacts, and habits stay on your phone. Cloud (Gemini) is only used as a fallback for open-ended conversation.
