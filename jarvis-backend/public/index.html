Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jarvis AI free?

Yes — completely free and open source. You can read every line of code on GitHub.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes for most commands — calls, SMS, apps, flashlight, volume, alarms, and more all run entirely on-device. Internet is only used as a fallback for open-ended conversation.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Your contacts, notes, and habits stay stored locally on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless a command specifically needs the cloud fallback (general conversation only).

What Android version do I need?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer.

Can I contribute?

Absolutely — it's open source. Pull requests, issues, and feature ideas are all welcome on GitHub.
